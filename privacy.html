Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to AIPetCo YouTube Ops (the “Application”) and its informational website. The Application is a private tool operated by the owner of the AiPetCo YouTube channel. Contact: videostedlv@gmail.com.

1. Purpose and scope

The Application supports the operator’s own channel-management and publishing workflow. It is not offered as a public service for third-party accounts. Google API access occurs only after explicit authorization by the account owner, and only within the permissions granted. This website itself does not perform Google authorization.

2. Information accessed and processed

  • Authorization information: OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, and granted permissions used to authenticate requests to Google APIs. The Application does not request or store Google account passwords.
  • YouTube channel and video information: channel identifiers, video identifiers, titles, descriptions, thumbnails, publication settings, and other metadata needed for authorized channel operations.
  • Publishing materials: video files, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and scheduling information prepared by the operator.
  • Analytics, where authorized: channel and video performance metrics returned by the YouTube Analytics API for the operator’s channel.
  • Support correspondence: information voluntarily provided when contacting the operator by email.

The actual Google consent screen determines which permissions are requested. This policy does not grant additional permissions. The Application does not solicit personal information from video viewers or request access to their Google accounts.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the operator, identify the authorized channel, upload and manage its own videos, maintain operational records, evaluate its own channel performance where permitted, and respond to support or privacy inquiries. Google user data is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Storage and security

The Application’s operational files and authorization tokens are stored on the operator’s computer. Authorized requests are transmitted to Google over HTTPS. Tokens and private operational records must not be published in this website or its public GitHub repository. Access is limited to the operator and authorized operational support where necessary. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

5. Disclosure and third-party services

Information and media necessary to carry out an authorized action are transmitted to Google and YouTube. Uploaded videos and their metadata may become visible according to the publication settings selected by the operator. Google user data is not otherwise shared for advertising or data-broker purposes. Disclosure may occur when required by applicable law or necessary to protect legal rights.

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Requests to the website are handled by GitHub, which may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under its own Privacy Statement. These pages contain no application-installed analytics scripts, advertising scripts, forms, or tracking cookies. External links are subject to the destination service’s policies.

6. Retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are retained while access is needed for the operator’s workflow. Operational records and publishing materials are retained only as needed for channel operations, troubleshooting, and applicable legal obligations. When access is discontinued, the operator should remove stored authorization tokens and Google-derived data that are no longer needed.

To request deletion of locally retained Google-derived information or support correspondence, email videostedlv@gmail.com. The operator will respond and process applicable requests, subject to necessary identity verification and legal retention requirements. Deleting local records does not automatically delete videos already uploaded to YouTube; those must be managed separately in YouTube.

7. Control and revocation

The account owner can revoke access at any time through Google Account permissions. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not itself erase previously retained local records or published content. Contact the operator to request local deletion.

8. Google API Limited Use

AIPetCo YouTube Ops’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Human access to Google user data is limited to the operator’s authorized workflow and circumstances permitted by that policy.

Google and YouTube handle information under their own Privacy Policy.

9. Changes and contact

Material changes to data-handling practices will be reflected on this page before the revised practices are adopted. For privacy questions or requests, contact videostedlv@gmail.com.
