AIPetCo YouTube Ops

AIPetCo YouTube Ops is a private publishing and channel-management tool operated by the owner of the AiPetCo YouTube channel. AiPetCo creates English-language AI-generated animal entertainment videos.

The application helps the channel owner prepare video uploads and manage the channel’s own content using the YouTube Data API. It is not a public service and is not offered to third-party channel owners.

This website provides the application’s identity, purpose, support details, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service for users reviewing its Google OAuth consent screen.

Publishing workflow

The operator prepares video files, titles, descriptions, and thumbnails for the AiPetCo channel. Authorized application functionality can include uploading videos and managing the channel’s own video information.

Owner-controlled access

Google account access requires the account owner’s explicit authorization. Permissions shown on the Google consent screen determine the access granted to the application. No Google authorization is required to read this website.

Google and YouTube access

AIPetCo YouTube Ops uses Google OAuth to obtain permission for its channel-management functions. It requests access for the operator’s authorized account, not for viewers of AiPetCo videos.

  • Google and YouTube data are used to operate the authorized channel-management workflow.
  • This informational website does not ask for Google passwords or accept authorization tokens.
  • The operator can revoke the application’s access through Google Account permissions.

See the Privacy Policy for information about data handling and the Terms of Service for use conditions.
